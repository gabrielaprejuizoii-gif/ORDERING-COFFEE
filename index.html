Step 1 · Grammar

Countable or uncountable?

Carrie counts her shoes. But she can’t count her coffee!

Countable ✔️

You can count: 1, 2, 3…
a croissanttwo bagelsan egg
Use a / an and the plural: a cupcake → three cupcakes

Uncountable ✖️

You can’t count: no plural.
coffeemilksugarwater
Use a measure: a cup of coffee, a glass of water, a slice of cake

Challenge: choose!

Step 2 · Grammar

Some & Any

some

Positive sentences
I’d like some coffee.
We have some muffins.

any

Questions and negatives
Do you have any milk?
We don’t have any bagels.
💡 Offers and polite requests use some: Would you like some cake?

Challenge: some or any?
